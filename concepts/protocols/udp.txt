
UDP - user datagram protocol, transport layer
connectionless, unreliable, unordered, no flow/congestion control

no handshake - just send "datagrams", fire and forget
tiny header (8 bytes) vs tcp's 20+ bytes -> less overhead

no guarantee of:
- delivery (packet can just be dropped, nobody retries)
- order (packets can arrive in a different order than sent)
- no duplicate detection

if the app needs any of that, IT has to implement it itself on top of udp

why use it: speed / low latency matters more than perfect reliability, or the app already
handles reliability itself in a way that fits its use case better than tcp's generic approach

used by: dns queries, video/audio streaming, voip, online games, quic (http/3 is built on udp,
quic adds its own reliability layer in userspace instead of relying on the kernel's tcp stack)

pros: low latency, low overhead, no head-of-line blocking, supports broadcast/multicast
cons: app has to handle reliability/ordering itself if it cares
