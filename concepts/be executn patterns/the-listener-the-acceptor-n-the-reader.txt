the listener, the acceptor, and the reader - 3 roles

Listener
- owns the listening socket (bound + listening on a port)
- mostly passive - exists so the os has somewhere to park completed handshakes (the backlog)

Acceptor
- the thread/code that calls accept() to pull a new connection off the listener's backlog
- hands the resulting per-connection socket off somewhere - to itself (to read), or to
  another thread/pool

Reader
- the thread/code that reads (recv/read) application data off an ALREADY-accepted
  connection's socket, and processes/responds to it

these 3 roles can all live on the SAME thread (simplest - pattern A) or be split across
DIFFERENT threads (more scalable, more complex - needs safe hand-off of sockets/data between
threads)

the rest of this module = every reasonable way to combine/split these 3 roles across threads,
and the tradeoff each choice makes
